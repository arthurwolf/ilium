### Added
- Right-clicking a shared resource in the tree adds a section for that resource: open its control panel, edit, pause or resume the queue, mark the hardware offline, remove it from the project, and delete it.
- Deleting a resource asks for confirmation in plain language, explains why it is refused when agents still use it or other projects share it, and removes it from the project then deletes it in one step.
- Resource control panel with a header and status badge, summary cards (status, holder, waiting, busy share of the last 24 hours), a queue table, a graph of recent hold times, a grouped activity log and details, and grouped buttons that show tooltips on hover and explain why a disabled button cannot be used.

### Changed
- Resource wording is now plain language ("Take back ownership", "Confirm safe", "Pause queue", "Mark offline", "Delete") in the panel, dialogs and help.
