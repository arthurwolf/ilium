### Changed
- "3D pipes", "Atlantic dusk", "Machine screen" and "Hex expedition" are now the `pipes`, `atlantic-dusk`, `machine-screen` and `hex-expedition` scenes of the Scenes plugin instead of built-in code; saved settings that name `pipes`, `atlantic_dusk`, `machine_screen` or `hex_expedition` open the Plugin scene animation. Their scene controls are the same except that rows whose names clash with the shared look are renamed (Brightness and Contrast became Intensity or Tile light, the scene's own Dither became Banding, Dither scale became Cell scale); colour, palette, dithering and display come from the shared rows. Disabling the Scenes plugin removes them from the list.
