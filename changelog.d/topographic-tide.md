### Changed
- Topographic maps: water is now tinted by depth below the water surface over a fixed 600 m, so a large tide no longer leaves a light/dark edge at the old continental shelf. The tide range goes up to 20000 m.

### Added
- Topographic maps: a Tide pattern setting. "Same everywhere" keeps one global sea level; "Travelling bulges" gives two tidal bulges that circle the world, so tide differs by longitude and latitude.

### Fixed
- Topographic maps: while the tide range is above 0 or the zero level is shifted, sea-floor contour lines are hidden. They used to bunch up on the continental slope and leave a fixed outline of the old coasts next to the moving shoreline.
