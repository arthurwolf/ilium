### Changed
- The key reference is now drawn only by the Keys and help plugin. `?` after the prefix, the voice "open help" action, F1 and the command palette all open its scrolling `keys` panel in the right panel instead of a full-screen help screen. The panel also lists what the mouse does and how to reach the terminal history, and it has no close hint of its own because the panel closes like any other. With the plugin turned off, those keys show "ilium.core.keys-and-help is not running" and no reference opens.

### Removed
- The built-in full-screen help screen.
