# What a recap sends

A recap is a short summary of what happened in one or more panes. To write it, Ilium
sends text from those panes to the language model you configured under Settings,
tab Inference.

- Only the panes you asked a recap for are read.
- The text goes to the configured provider and nowhere else.
- No recap is made automatically unless automatic AI features are switched on under
  Inference and privacy.

Terminal text can contain secrets, such as a token printed by a command. Do not ask for a
recap of a pane that shows something you would not send to the provider.
